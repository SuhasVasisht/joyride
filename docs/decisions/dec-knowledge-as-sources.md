# Knowledge is sources, not distillates

Date: 2026-10-05
State: accepted
Supersedes: [dec-firstmate-memory-single-store](dec-firstmate-memory-single-store.md), [dec-hillclimb-backpass-jev](dec-hillclimb-backpass-jev.md), [dec-learnings-on-demand-nudged](dec-learnings-on-demand-nudged.md)

## Decision

Keep sources findable through artifact cards, notes and search. Keep procedures in skills and repo facts in repo instructions and docs. Stop distilling artifacts into a separate knowledge store.

## Why

The first-principles review found that distillation produces lossy copies that agents rarely read and that can drift from the original source. Authored intent and direct source links retain what people and agents need.
