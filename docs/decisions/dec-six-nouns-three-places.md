# Six nouns and three places for guidance

Date: 2026-10-05
State: accepted
Supersedes: [dec-workspace-as-code](dec-workspace-as-code.md), [dec-ideas-auto-tidy](dec-ideas-auto-tidy.md)

## Decision

Organize work as Idea, Group, Note, Session, Task and Artifact. Keep guidance in repo AGENTS.md/CLAUDE.md and docs/, the skills/ tree, and the captain's preference file. Keep evidence records as proof. Idea housekeeping files unplaced Firstmate tasks into ideas with pins and Undo. The captain controls renames, merges and archives. Map settings remain read-only; session launch starts a normal session with the idea's notes and artifact links.

## Why

People and agents can read the same authored files and follow the same links. These concepts describe work and its outputs without adding a second knowledge lifecycle or a separate agent mode.
